# Corporate allocator research -- 2026-W48 (2026-11-23)

## What moved this week

The week's corporate capital-allocation story is almost entirely one event: **Anthropic's
IPO and concurrent private placement**, which closed 2026-11-19 after pricing at $83/share
(above the $70-78 range) and raising $81.3B -- the largest IPO on record. Three of our six
watchlist names bought into the $26.0B concurrent private placement of Class N (non-voting)
stock, confirmed to the share and dollar in Anthropic's own SEC 424B4 prospectus:

- **NVIDIA** -- $10.0B (120,481,927 shares)
- **Alphabet** (via Google LLC) -- $10.0B (120,481,927 shares)
- **Microsoft** -- $1.0B (12,048,192 shares)

The other four slots in that placement went to AMD ($2.5B), Broadcom ($1.0B), Micron
($1.0B) and TSMC ($0.5B) -- all now flagged in discovered_allocators.csv, since this is the
first week we've seen that specific group of chip/hardware suppliers taking direct equity
in a shared AI-lab customer alongside our existing hyperscaler/silicon watchlist.

Separately, **Alphabet** also confirmed (via Isomorphic Labs' own press release) continued
participation, through GV and CapitalG, in Isomorphic Labs' $5.0B Series C (co-led by
Greenoaks and Sequoia, announced 2026-11-17). Alphabet's specific dollar amount was not
disclosed, so it is filed verified-but-unquantified rather than with a guessed amount_usd.

**Amazon** is notably absent from the Anthropic placement itself -- its (very large) stake
is existing preferred/convertible-note conversion recorded as of 2026-09-30, not new money
moved this week, so no row was filed for it (filing one would have doubled-counted an old
position as a new event).

## The 2-3 biggest signals

1. **Chip/hardware suppliers are becoming direct equity investors in their own AI-lab
   customers, in size.** NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom, Micron and TSMC all bought into the same
   placement in one week -- not just Nvidia's familiar pattern of taking stakes in
   customers, but now spread across the whole compute supply chain.
2. **Alphabet and NVIDIA are matched at $10B apiece into Anthropic**, each roughly 10x
   Microsoft's $1B ticket -- a visible signal of where the two are placing relative bets
   in the frontier-lab race versus their OpenAI/Microsoft-anchored rival camp.
3. **Governance shock at NVIDIA**: an 8-K (Item 5.02, filed 2026-11-12) confirms Jensen
   Huang's resignation as CEO effective immediately, with CFO Colette Kress named interim
   CEO and director Harvey Jones named Chair. Not a capital-allocation event itself (so no
   CSV row), but it lands in the same week NVIDIA committed $10B to Anthropic and is
   material context for reading that commitment and any forward moves out of NVentures.
